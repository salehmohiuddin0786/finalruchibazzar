1. Distance-Based Delivery Fee Schedule

Customer delivery charges are calculated strictly based on road transit distance between the customer address and the restaurant. 100% of this fee is allocated directly to the delivery partner:

Distance Radius Customer Delivery Charge Delivery Partner Receives Pricing Logic
≤ 5.0 km (Under 5 km) ₹15.00 ₹15.00 (100%) Flat base rate for all neighborhood deliveries
5.1 km – 6.0 km (6 km) ₹18.00 ₹18.00 (100%) Base ₹15 + ₹3 distance increment
6.1 km – 7.0 km (7 km) ₹21.00 ₹21.00 (100%) Base ₹15 + ₹6 distance increment
Beyond 7.0 km ₹21.00 + ₹3 / km Full collected fee ₹3 for each additional kilometer

3. Settlement & Payout Guarantees
  • Zero Delivery Deficit: 100% of distance-based delivery fees collected from customers are credited to the delivery partner's wallet.
  • Restaurant Settlement Requests: Restaurants can request withdrawals at any time once their accrued balance reaches ₹100. Payouts are transferred via NEFT/IMPS/UPI.
  • Verified Bank UTR: No payout is marked as confirmed without a valid Bank UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) number recorded on the main administration ledger.
  • Order Cancellation Protection: If an order is cancelled before fulfillment, accrued earnings are automatically reversed to prevent erroneous overpayments.
